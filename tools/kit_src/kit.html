How to

Groups are labelled boxes, such as Ingredients. Drag cards into one, then draw a line from the group to a machine and call it input or output. Groups and their lines live on your board only and never change the game's sheets. Drag a card to move it. Drag from the dot on a card's right edge onto another card to connect them; the builder tells you which CSV cell that writes. Click a card or line, then press Delete (or the red button) to remove it.
