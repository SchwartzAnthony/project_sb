Prompts built with: godot --headless --path . --script res://tools/art_prompt.gd -- ludo "<SUBJECT>" and passed verbatim.
augment_prompt left at default (true). Raw generations (.webp + _gen.png) are kept in raw/.

Cut-outs: every cut-out was then run through removeBackground {creative_edit: false, crop: true}
(the "sprite" generations already came back RGBA-transparent; removeBackground just cleaned/cropped them).
Downloads: mcp__openai-images__download_file; .webp converted to PNG with Pillow.

Style reference (crowd, brawl, hero): Ludo takes style_image as URL or base64 only (no local file / upload).
Passed art_source/style_refs/stammtisch/board_characters.png as base64, downscaled to a 256x128 JPEG q45
(12 KB of base64) so it fits in a tool call. Ludo accepted it.
