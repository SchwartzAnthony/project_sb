== How it was made ==
Every layer is a hand-written SVG (bg, brewery, tent, hero by hand; sign,
crowd, brawl generated by small Python scripts in this folder that write the
SVG paths: crowd_build.py, brawl_build.py, people.py = shared comic face/fist/
stein parts). Rendered with: rsvg-convert -w <width> <name>.svg -o <name>.png
Outlines: #141414, 10-20 px so they survive the half-size pixelation.
One shadow tone per shape, no hatching, neutral palette.
Fix rounds: bg (mountains/trees redrawn, mowing bands), brewery (crooked walls,
bent roof, grass base), tent (flags), sign (back edge), hero (limb colours,
arm/stein raised, feather), crowd (one row, faces in front of arms),
brawl (bodies under heads, hair behind faces, flying items spread).
