PixelLab set (round AN title-screen tool comparison)
=====================================================
Tool:  PixelLab MCP, create_image_pro ("Create Image (Pro)") for all 7 layers.
       Chosen because it is PixelLab's best-quality text-to-image model, the
       largest canvas (688x384 at 16:9, 384x688 at 9:16, 512x512 square) and
       the only one that takes a style image at those sizes.
Cost:  40 generations per call, 8 calls = 320 subscription generations
       (no USD credits; balance before: 4637 generations remaining).
Prompts: built with  godot --headless --path . --script res://tools/art_prompt.gd -- pixellab "<SUBJECT>"
       and passed verbatim (no length limit hit, nothing shortened).
Style image (crowd, brawl, hero only): art_source/style_refs/stammtisch/board_characters.png,
       downscaled to 192x96 and quantised to 24 colours (no dither) so it fits
       inline as style_image_base64 (PixelLab has no upload tool and no public URL
       was available). style_copy = default (color_palette, outline, detail, shading).
Seed:  unset (random) except the sign retry (seed 7).
